# Orrery style guide

## Transitions
- No part pierces straight into another. Where two parts meet, there is
  a collar, a hub or a fillet.
- All shafts and tubes are turned profiles with a shoulder and a
  chamfered end. Refer to the moodboard

## Arms
-- Arms are parallel, with rounded ends, for a calm, technical line.
- Arms meet the hub with a soft transition, never a sharp corner.

## Panels
- Surfaces are divided by thin, dark seams, like the panel gaps on a car
  or the joints in a triangulated facade.
- Planets are geodesic spheres: triangular panels with narrow seams.

## Accents
- Red is used sparingly: the sun, and a thin ring where each planet post
  meets its arm.
- No colours other than white, dark metal and red

## Cogs
- The arms in the cogs meet the edge with a smooth fillet
